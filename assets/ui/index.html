Pulse Floor+

Welcome to Pulse Floor+

Survive as long as possible on the shifting floor as pulse waves try to remove tiles beneath your feet!

⚡

The Challenge

Avoid standing on tiles when the pulse passes over them, or they'll vanish beneath your feet! The challenge increases over time with new pulse types and special tiles.

🏆

Your Goal

Stay alive as long as possible, scoring points by surviving and avoiding pulses.

🎮

Controls

WASD to move, SPACE to jump, and RIGHT-CLICK or E to use your feather jump! Use MOUSE WHEEL to zoom out for a better view.

Special Tiles

Watch out for these special tiles that can affect your movement and survival!

Cracked Tiles

These weakened tiles will break and vanish after you step on them, even without a pulse!

Sticky Tiles

Standing on these will slow your movement for a few seconds, making it harder to dodge pulses!

Fake Tiles

These deceptive tiles may randomly vanish beneath you at any moment, without warning!

Bonus Tiles

Rare special tiles that give you extra points when collected. Watch for the notification!

Pulse Types

Different pulse waves will appear as the game progresses. Learn to avoid them all!

Standard Pulse

Expands outward from the center altar. Any tile it passes over will glow red and disappear if you're standing on it!

Reverse Pulse

Starts from the edge and contracts toward the center. Appears in Phase 3 and higher.

Cross Pulse

Moves in straight lines across the platform. Appears in Phase 4 and higher.

Random Pulse

Unpredictable patterns that can come from any direction. Appears in later phases.
